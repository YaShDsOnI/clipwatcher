# Code of conduct

clipwatcher should be a friendly place to ask questions, report bugs and contribute, whatever your
experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first, and share the details needed to help you.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Using the project to target, harass or dox streamers or their communities.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, Yash Soni ([@YaShDsOnI](https://github.com/YaShDsOnI)), may edit or remove comments,
issues and contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously do. To
report a problem privately, use **Report content** on the comment or issue (GitHub forwards it to the
maintainer), or open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab.

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews. It is inspired by
the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
